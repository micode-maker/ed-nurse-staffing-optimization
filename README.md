# ED Nurse Staffing Optimization Engine

## Overview
The goal of this project is to develop an optimization engine that can evaluate the 120 possible roster variations for nurses working in a large emergency department in Auckland. By identifying the Pareto frontier of efficient solutions,  it lays the foundations for a decision agent to be developed to recommend the most optimal staffing levels.

## Business Problem
Health New Zealand | Te Whatu Ora have a target of 95% of ED patients to be discharged within 6 hours. In March 2026 only 74.4% were discharged with a lack of around 2000 registered nurses in hospitals. As adding more nurses to the team will increase patient flow but also cost more money ED managers have to make a decision on how many core and floating nurses they want to roster per shift.
